# Dar Global source coverage

Snapshot reviewed on 23 October 2026. Thirty readable pages were collected; three duplicate product-category routes were removed, leaving 27 distinct URLs, with 23 English and four Arabic pages. Wasalt was not fetched.

The authorization basis is the company-issued FDE assignment plus the user’s explicit instruction to proceed for this assessment. Dar’s [terms](https://darglobal.co.uk/terms-conditions) reserve copying/transmission rights; this record does not assert a separate website licence or a legal determination. The private record retains the scope; no recruitment email or attachment is published.

## Collection evidence and limits

- Honest user agent `AssessmentResearchBot/1.0`; sequential requests with at least two seconds between requests; robots checked before page requests.
- Same-host HTTPS directory scope; redirects validated; no off-site brochures, login, CAPTCHA or anti-bot bypass.
- Homepage, Arabic homepage, investor page, Arabic investor page, Arabic projects landing and Qatar landing returned insufficient readable HTML and were skipped. The corpus is therefore mainly project pages, not a complete company history.
- Three `/product-category/` copies were removed in favor of the canonical project URLs. Whitespace and Unicode separators were normalized. Arabic source titles were made readable where the page title was missing or English.
- No email/phone-pattern matches were found in the retained text. Forms, navigation, headers, footers, scripts and SVGs were excluded. This automated scan plus agent sampling does not guarantee the absence of every personal detail.
- Both language versions are publisher sources, not machine-translated pages. Coverage is uneven: four Arabic projects versus 23 English pages; multilingual retrieval can also answer Arabic questions from English evidence.
- Source descriptions, sizes and FAQ statements can conflict even within a page. Source marketing, investment/visa/tax claims and future dates are not independently verified advice. Preserve dates, report conflicts and abstain where evidence is missing.
- Raw HTML, full text, hashes, collection times, review report, vectors and deployment bundle are ignored private artifacts under `data/private/dar/`. Public metadata below is a coverage inventory, not a full-text download.
